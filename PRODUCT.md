# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Kenyan residential rehabilitation staff: administrators, clinicians, nurses, counsellors, finance staff and auditors. They work on desktop and tablet in an institutional environment and need clear, attributable records.

## Product Purpose

ARS RMS supports the rehabilitation journey while preserving one permanent client record across admissions. The current deliverable is Phase 1: secure identity, authorization, audit and administration infrastructure.

## Brand Personality

Professional, restrained, accountable. Requirements are taken directly from the supplied project specification.

## Anti-references

No consumer styling, artwork, gradients, glass, decorative animation, neon colours, oversized headings or excessive shadows.

## Design Principles

- Make operational state explicit; never invent clinical metrics.
- Enforce least privilege and separate ICT administration from clinical access.
- Prefer familiar Bootstrap forms, tables and navigation.
- Show errors, loading and empty states clearly.
- Keep planned modules visibly unavailable until implemented.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML, labelled controls, keyboard navigation, visible focus, accessible contrast, responsive offcanvas navigation and errors expressed in text.
