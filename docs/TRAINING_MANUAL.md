# ReHUB Staff Training Manual

Welcome to the ReHUB Training Program! This manual is designed to give you a hands-on, step-by-step introduction to the Rehabilitation Management System. 

Depending on your role (Admissions, Clinical, Nursing, or Finance), you may only need to complete specific modules.

---

## Module 1: System Basics & Navigation
**Target Audience:** All Staff

### 1.1 Logging In
1. Open your web browser and navigate to the ReHUB portal URL.
2. Enter your assigned **Email Address** and **Password**.
3. *Note:* If you are inactive for 30 minutes, the system will securely log you out.

### 1.2 Dashboard Overview
When you log in, you will see a personalized dashboard based on your role.
- **Navigation Sidebar (Left):** Use this to jump between modules (Admissions, Clinical, Finance, Reports).
- **Crisis Alerts (Top/Center):** Clinicians will see active, unacknowledged crisis alerts here.
- **User Menu (Top Right):** Access your profile or log out.

---

## Module 2: Admissions & Front Desk
**Target Audience:** Admissions Coordinators, Administrators

### Exercise 1: Logging a New Referral
1. Click on **Admissions** in the sidebar.
2. Click the **+ New Referral** button.
3. Fill in the client's basic details (Name, Contact, Reason for Referral).
4. Click **Save**. The client now exists in the system as a pre-admission referral.

### Exercise 2: Admitting a Client
1. From the Referral view, click **Admit Client**.
2. Assign the client to a **Ward/Room** and select the **Attending Clinician**.
3. Submit the form. The system will automatically create a unique, permanent **Client Record** and an active **Admission Record**.

---

## Module 3: Clinical Care & Crisis Management
**Target Audience:** Clinicians, Counsellors, Psychiatrists

### Exercise 3: Administering the C-SSRS Assessment
1. Navigate to the **Clinical** tab and select your active patient.
2. Go to the **Assessments** sub-tab and click **New Assessment**.
3. Select **C-SSRS (Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale)** from the dropdown.
4. Go through the standardized questionnaire with the client, marking "Yes" or "No".
5. Click **Submit**. The system will automatically calculate the risk level.

### Exercise 4: Handling a Crisis Alert
If a client scores "Moderate" or "High Risk" on the C-SSRS, an alert is triggered.
1. Return to your **Dashboard**.
2. Locate the **Active Crisis Alerts** widget. You will see a red/orange banner for the high-risk client.
3. Once you have stabilized the patient or initiated the safety protocol, click the **Acknowledge** button next to the alert. This removes it from the active queue and logs your intervention in the audit trail.

## Module 4: Nursing & Daily Care
**Target Audience:** Nurses, Care Workers

### Exercise 5: Logging Daily Vitals
1. Click on **Nursing** in the sidebar.
2. Select the client's name from the active ward list.
3. Click **Add Vitals** and input the current Blood Pressure, Heart Rate, and Temperature.
4. Click **Save**. *Remember: Once saved, clinical records are locked and cannot be directly edited.*

### Exercise 6: Correcting an Error (The Revision Workflow)
Because ReHUB enforces strict medical data integrity, you cannot "delete" a mistake.
1. Locate the erroneous vitals entry and click **Revise**.
2. Enter the corrected values.
3. You **must** provide a reason for the revision (e.g., "Entered data for the wrong patient").
4. Submit. The system will now show the new data, but auditors can still trace the original entry.

---

## Module 5: Billing & Finance
**Target Audience:** Finance Officers, Billing Clerks

### Exercise 7: Generating an Invoice
1. Navigate to the **Finance** module.
2. Click **Create Invoice** and search for the admitted client.
3. Add line items (e.g., "Standard 30-Day Program", "Detox Medication").
4. **Issue Invoice**. The total outstanding balance for the client is updated.

### Exercise 8: Logging an M-Pesa Payment
1. From the generated Invoice, click **Receive Payment**.
2. Select **M-Pesa** as the payment method.
3. Enter the exact **M-Pesa Transaction Code** and the amount received.
4. Click **Confirm**. The invoice balance will automatically decrease.
